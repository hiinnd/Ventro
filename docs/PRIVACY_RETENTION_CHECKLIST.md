# Privacy and data-retention checklist

## Decisions required from the business/legal owner

- [ ] Identify data controller/legal operator, Jordan/MENA processing basis, privacy contact, and approved English/Arabic policy text.
- [ ] Define retention/deletion schedules for accounts, sessions, audit logs, invitations, registration answers, tickets/check-ins, questions/polls, certificates, Passports, uploads, backups, and email-delivery records.
- [ ] Define user rights workflow for access, correction, deletion, export, objection, and consent withdrawal.
- [ ] Approve processor agreements for hosting, email, monitoring, storage, AI, and payment providers.
- [ ] Establish breach assessment/notification ownership and timelines with counsel.

## Product safeguards to retain

- Passport visibility remains opt-in; do not reveal private profile, attendance, or certificate data without the applicable setting.
- QR payloads must remain opaque and exclude personal data.
- Audit logs and certificates require controlled retention: they can be security/legal evidence but must not be kept indefinitely by default.
- AI must remain disabled unless the provider, input classes, consent, retention, and review workflow are approved. Never transmit participant personal data without authorization.
- Pilot exports and feedback should use the minimum necessary data, restricted access, and a deletion date.

No retention period is assumed by this checklist; implementation must follow approved policy and applicable legal advice.
